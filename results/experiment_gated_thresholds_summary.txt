Compute gated aggregation metrics at c_local >= 0.95 and c_local = 1.00 with

DATA:

- Sentinel per-sample: 10,958 samples (sentinel_per_sample.csv)
- Label distribution: 3,404 attacks (31.1%), 7,554 benign (68.9%)

================================================================================
GATED AGGREGATION LOGIC
================================================================================

For each sample:
  if stage4_says_attack (Sentinel=1):
      final = attack (1)
  elif local_says_attack AND local_conf >= gate_threshold:
      final = attack (1)  [OVERRIDE - local overrides Stage 4]
  else:
      final = stage4_decision

Where:
  - "local" = Stages 1-3 combined (full_pred, full_conf columns)
  - "stage4" = Sentinel (sentinel_pred column)

================================================================================
INTERPRETATION
================================================================================

1. OVERRIDE BEHAVIOR:
   - At c_local >= 0.90: 218 local overrides, 11.93% precision
   - At c_local >= 0.95: 193 local overrides, 4.15% precision
   - At c_local = 1.00: 0 local overrides (perfect confidence not achieved)
   
   Note: As confidence threshold increases, overrides decrease (218→193→0), 
   but precision becomes MUCH WORSE (11.93%→4.15%), indicating that Stages 1-3
   rarely reach very high confidence AND are correct.

2. SENTINEL DOMINANCE:
   - At c_local = 1.00, the system becomes Sentinel-only (no overrides)
   - This recovers Sentinel's full performance: F1=0.9209
   - Stages 1-3 contributions at high confidence are not reliable

3. SUPPRESSION BEHAVIOR:
   - Stage 4 suppressions increase as threshold increases (51→76→269)
   - Suppression precision improves at c_local >= 0.95 (33.33%→46.05%)
   - At c_local = 1.00, 269 suppressions have only 15.99% precision (many FNs)
   
4. FALSE POSITIVES:
   - All threshold configurations reduce FPs compared to pure combination:
     * c_local >= 0.90: 355 FPs
     * c_local >= 0.95: 348 FPs
     * c_local = 1.00: 163 FPs (Sentinel-only baseline)

5. OPTIMAL CONFIGURATION:
   - c_local = 1.00 (Sentinel-only) achieves best precision (0.9492)
   - Maintains recall at 0.8942, F1 at 0.9209
   - This is equivalent to "Stage 4 Tripwire" + Sentinel
   - Local stages (1-3) do not contribute meaningfully when gating is strict
